Welcome, future teacher!

Your journey to DSC success starts here. Improve your English grammar and vocabulary with smart practice tools, questions from previous exams, and learning support designed for you.

Special Features

🎯 Practice by Topic

Choose a topic you want to improve and practise it until you feel confident. The app covers 17 broad areas and 59 subtopics.

Tenses & Aspect, Prepositions, Sentence Transformation, Articles, Modals & Semi-Modals, Spelling, Reported Speech, Punctuation

+20 more topics

All questions are authentic and taken from actual previous exam papers.

🎚️ Choose Difficulty Level

Choose the difficulty level for each topic: Low, Medium, or High. Start with easier questions, build your confidence, and move gradually to more difficult ones.

💡 Smart Feedback

In many practice apps, your answer is immediately marked right or wrong, and the correct answer is shown. You may also be told why the other options are wrong. This gives you little chance to think again and find the answer yourself.

This special app works differently. If you choose a wrong option, the feedback helps you understand why it may be wrong without immediately giving you the correct answer. You can then think again and choose from the remaining options. By trying again, you learn the rule, tip, or trick behind the question instead of simply memorising the answer.

⏱️ Previous Papers

Practise with 1,418 authentic language questions from 80 previous-paper sets.

You can also use Practice Mode with previous papers. Attempt real exam questions without a time limit and get feedback on each option as you practise.

🧠 Remedial Tests

After a normal test, you usually see your score and review your answers once. Later, you may not get another chance to practise the questions you answered incorrectly.

This special app remembers the questions you get wrong in every test and collects them for you.

After completing 5 or 10 tests, open Remedial Tests to practise those questions again. This helps you check whether you have really mastered them.

App Instructions

📱 How to Save to Home Screen

  • iPhone (Safari): Tap the 'Share' icon at the bottom of the screen (a square with an upward arrow). Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): Tap the three-dot menu icon in the top right corner. Select Add to Home Screen or Install App.

🧭 Navigation Guide

  • Start at the Main Menu to pick your activity.
  • Inside a session, use Prev and Next to move between questions, or tap List to jump to any specific question number.
  • Always use the red Quit button or the Return to Dashboard button to exit. Do not use your phone's browser back button!

✅ Do's and Don'ts

  • DO take the Remedial Test regularly to clear out your logged mistakes.
  • DO submit your test to save your progress and log your errors.
  • DON'T refresh the webpage while taking a timed test, or your progress will be lost.

💡 Pro Tips

  • In Practice Mode: Don't rush! Read the feedback carefully. If you get it wrong, the app will give you a hint without revealing the answer. Use that hint to try again!
  • In Testing Mode: Treat it like the real DSC exam. Keep an eye on the clock. If you get stuck, use the 'List' button to quickly skip and return to difficult questions later.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

  • App not loading: Make sure you have an internet connection the very first time you open the app. After that, it works completely offline!
  • Icon disappeared: If you accidentally delete the app from your home screen, simply visit the web link again and repeat the "Add to Home Screen" steps.
